A vision-language model fits <span class="eq">p(text | o, l)</span>: it can say that the mug will fall, but it has no output space for an action and no generative model of what the pixels do next. A video diffusion model fits <span class="eq">p(o<sub>t+1:T</sub> | o<sub>≤t</sub>, l)</span> on logged video, which marginalises over whatever policy produced the data, <span class="eq">Σ<sub>a</sub> p(o′ | o, a) π<sub>data</sub>(a | o)</span>; with no action variable it cannot answer the interventional question of what happens under <span class="eq">do(a)</span>.
